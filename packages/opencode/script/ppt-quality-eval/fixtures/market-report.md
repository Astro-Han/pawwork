# Field Service AI Market Report

## Core Insight

Field-service teams are adopting AI assistants fastest where dispatch, billing, and customer messaging are already digitized. The buying trigger is not "AI novelty"; it is reducing missed appointments and truck-roll waste.

## Evidence

- Dispatch teams lose 8-12 hours per week to manual rescheduling.
- 64% of surveyed operators already use at least one SaaS scheduling tool.
- Buyers trust AI most when it drafts actions but leaves approval with the dispatcher.
- The strongest wedge is exception handling: weather delays, parts shortages, customer no-shows.

## Decision

Prioritize an AI exception-handling workflow over a broad "field-service copilot" launch. The first deck should show the workflow clearly enough for a VP Operations audience to approve a prototype.
